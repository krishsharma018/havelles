Split policy:
- Exact input overlap is forbidden across all splits.
- START_CYCLE semantic scenarios are split before text generation, so their target JSONs do not overlap.
- Train/validation/test use separate language-template families.
- Atomic and finite single-slot labels can repeat across splits because they are the actual labels the model must learn.
